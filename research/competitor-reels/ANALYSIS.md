## What to steal
**From saveo.house:**
- Second-person "you" POV, which makes the viewer the protagonist.
- Real, surprising facts used as obstacles, so viewers learn something while being entertained.
- A visible meter (Suspicion %) that only goes the wrong way.
- Numbered rules that are later broken.
- A binary vote CTA, with "the majority writes the next part".
- Each episode opens with the vote %, a line for the losing side, and a quoted top comment.
- Commenters credited in the caption ("Founders on the case board").
- A tease *after* the CTA ("There's a third way out… it's been in your pocket this whole episode"), which keeps people watching through the CTA and invites a rewatch to find it.

**From beanie.escape:**
- Cinematic dread built from ordinary things: pizza delivery, a polite grandma, a lock "being repaired".
- Rule-of-three escalation: "Another door opened. Then another."
- A recurring number and place (Apartment 304), a time (3 a.m.), and a character tic ("sweetheart").
- Endings where the villain is right behind you, and an ally who turns.

## What to avoid (beanie.escape's leaks)
- **An unpaid cliffhanger.** Part 1 ends with "I lived on the top floor" (someone dragging furniture above the top floor). Part 2 never mentions it. Viewers who notice stop trusting cliffhangers.
- **Caption and story disagree.** The Part 2 caption says "the cops seem to not wanna come", but in the script the police say someone will check.
- **Weak series navigation.** There's no "start with Part 1 (pinned)", and Part 3 can't be seen by everyone, which breaks the chain.
- **Captions with typos and no CTA** ("whatever they gave there it can't be good").
- **A horizontal 16:9 file in a vertical feed.** It probably wastes most of the screen.
