# YOU: the permanent main character (LOCKED)

He's the main character of **every** Midnight Parts story, the "you" the narration speaks to. He was first drawn for *The Ship Kept Going*, Part 1.

> **Locked.** Never redesign him or change his face, hair, proportions, drawing style or outfit unless the user explicitly asks.
> Each story changes only his **expressions, poses, surroundings and physical state** (wet, dirty, injured, cold).

## Reference images (attach to every image prompt he appears in)

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `you.png` | **Master turnaround sheet** (front, three-quarter, back). Always attach this one. |
| `you-wet.png` | Wet or in-water state (hair plastered flat, soaked jacket) |
| `example-standing-front.png` | On-model full body in a scene |
| `example-back.png` | On-model back view |
| `example-panic.png`, `example-shock.png` | On-model expression range (panic: open mouth; shock: big white eyes with tiny pupils) |

For every new series, copy `you.png` and `you-wet.png` into each part's `reference-images/` folder, next to that series' own characters and places.

## Locked design

| Feature | Exactly |
|---|---|
| Body | Stick figure: thin black stick neck, arms and legs, white cartoon hands |
| Head | Smooth white egg-shaped head, no nose and no ears |
| Hair | Short, messy, spiky black hair |
| Eyes | Small, simple black oval eyes. Thin black brows appear only to show expression. |
| Mouth | A simple line or small curve; opens for expressions |
| Jacket | Bright yellow (`#FFC72C`) zip-up windbreaker with a high collar and elastic cuffs and hem, worn **open** by default (zipping it up is allowed as a story action) |
| Shirt | Plain white crew-neck T-shirt |
| Shorts | Knee-length dark navy drawstring shorts |
| Shoes | Black flip-flops |
| Style | 2D hand-drawn cartoon, bold clean black ink outlines, simple flat shading, the same proportions as `you.png` |

The yellow jacket is the channel's signature. He's always the most saturated, brightest thing in the frame.

## Locked prompt phrases (paste these exactly, then add the expression, pose and scene)

**Dry (default)**
```
a stick-figure man with a smooth white egg-shaped head, short messy black hair, small simple black oval eyes, a bright yellow zip-up windbreaker with a high collar worn open over a plain white crew-neck T-shirt, knee-length dark navy drawstring shorts and black flip-flops, thin black stick arms and legs, white cartoon hands
```

**Wet**
```
a stick-figure man with a smooth white egg-shaped head, wet black hair plastered flat, small simple black oval eyes, a soaked bright yellow zip-up windbreaker worn open over a wet white crew-neck T-shirt, knee-length dark navy drawstring shorts, thin black stick arms and legs, white cartoon hands
```

**Close-up (head and shoulders only)**
```
a stick-figure man with a smooth white egg-shaped head, short messy black hair, small simple black oval eyes and the high collar of a bright yellow windbreaker
```

**Wet close-up**
```
a stick-figure man with a smooth white egg-shaped head, wet black hair plastered flat, small simple black oval eyes and the high collar of a soaked bright yellow windbreaker
```

Then follow with the expression, the action, the setting and light, and the fixed style tail from playbook §11.1.

## What a story may change

| Allowed ✅ | Not allowed without explicit instruction ❌ |
|---|---|
| Expressions (fear, shock, relief, anger, determination) | Face shape, eye style, hair style or colour |
| Poses and actions | Proportions or body type |
| Surroundings, light and weather | Any part of the outfit (no coats, uniforms, costumes or swaps) |
| Physical state: wet, muddy, torn sleeve, bruise, bandage, sweat, shivering | The jacket's colour (it's always the yellow accent) |
| Props he holds (phone, flashlight, rope) | Turning him into a different character, age or gender |

## Writing stories around him
- He's always the protagonist and always "you" in the voiceover. He's unnamed unless a story needs another character to call him something.
- Pick premises where he can plausibly be in his outfit: travel, holidays, a beach, a city at night, a road trip, a ship, an island. For snow or formal settings, either the story explains it (he's caught off guard, still in what he was wearing) or you ask the user first.
- A story may give the jacket a role (in series 001 it belonged to Marcus), but the jacket itself never changes.
- New supporting characters are designed per series in `series/NNN-slug/reference/character-prompts.md`. They must look clearly different from him: different hair, no yellow.
