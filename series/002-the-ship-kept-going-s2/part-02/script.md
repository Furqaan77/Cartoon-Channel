## Voiceover (paste into TTS)
<!-- VO:START -->
[low, tense, steady] A killer gave you seven days to find your brother. Then he drove away.

[firm, steady] Most of you said call.

[brisk, relieved] Agent Kerr picks up on the first ring. "Go," she says. "We'll be right behind you. He'll only come out for you."

[quick, matter-of-fact] An hour later, a courier brings a phone. "Use this one," her text says. "It's secure."

[knowing, a little uneasy] Go voters, you got your wish. She wanted you on that plane too.

[warm, then tense] Cancún. Eighty-six degrees. You're the only person in the airport wearing a jacket.

[wry, uneasy] Sweat runs down your back. You still don't take it off.

[slowing, curious] In the bathroom, you finally feel it. The collar is stiff. The stitching inside doesn't match. White thread, sewn by hand.

[tense, close] You pick it open with your nail. A strip of fabric. Twelve words. Marcus's tiny handwriting.

[reading, slow, puzzled] Not a message. Not an address. Just twelve random words.

[quick, factual, confident] Here's what you don't know. A crypto wallet can be opened with twelve words. Whoever has them can move the money. No bank. No password. No name.

[slow, stunned] Two million dollars. Stitched into your collar.

[cold realization] You weren't his decoy. You were his suitcase.

[measured, bitter] A man overboard gets rescued, wrapped in a blanket, and never searched. That note at 3:38 wasn't mercy. It was a pickup.

[uneasy, slowing] That's why you never took it off. They knew. They just didn't know where.

[sharp, a jolt] The new phone buzzes. A Mexican number.

[reading, quiet, urgent] "Isla Mujeres. Come alone. Rule three: never say the words out loud. M."

[direct, tense] Keep the strip, or memorize the words and burn it?

[lighter, inviting] Comment KEEP or BURN. The majority writes Part 3.
