## Character phrases (use these exact words in every prompt)
- **YOU:** the locked phrases in [`channel/characters/you/CHARACTER.md`](../../../channel/characters/you/CHARACTER.md), word for word.
- **MARCUS:** `a stick-figure man with a white egg-shaped head, short neat black hair, a short black beard, a navy button-up shirt with rolled sleeves and a gold watch`
- **TABLE 9 MAN:** `a broad-shouldered stick-figure man with a bald white egg-shaped head, heavy black brows, a flat expressionless mouth, a gray polo shirt and a chunky steel dive watch on his left wrist`
- **AGENT KERR:** `a stick-figure woman with a white egg-shaped head, short gray-streaked hair, round reading glasses, tired eyes, a dark blazer over a white shirt and a blue ID lanyard`
- **LUCÍA:** `a stick-figure woman with a white egg-shaped head, long curly dark-brown hair tied back with a red bandana, a teal rash-guard top, khaki cargo shorts and a shark-tooth necklace`
- **THE CAR:** `a dark gray sedan with tinted windows`

Nobody but YOU wears yellow.

## Colour and light
- **Home at night (Part 1):** blue-black, one streetlight, the porch light, phone glow.
- **Cancún (Part 2):** harsh white sun, crowded and over-bright; the airport bathroom in sickly fluorescent green.
- **Isla Mujeres (Parts 3–4):** turquoise water and golden-hour sun, then sodium-orange string lights at night.
- **At sea (Part 5):** black water, starlight, laptop glow. The yellow jacket is the only color.
