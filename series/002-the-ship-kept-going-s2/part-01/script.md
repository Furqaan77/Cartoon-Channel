## Voiceover (paste into TTS)
<!-- VO:START -->
[low, tense, almost a whisper] 2:14 a.m. A photo of your front door. From a number you don't know.

[whispering, cold] "From behind, you really do look like him."

[quick, steady, recapping] Three weeks ago, a man pushed you off a cruise ship. He thought you were your brother, Marcus. Marcus stole two million dollars and vanished.

[low, menacing] The man who pushed you wants it back.

[uneasy, slowing] Your phone buzzes again.

[reading, cold, clipped] "You never take that jacket off."

[hushed, unsettled] You look down. Marcus's yellow jacket. You've slept in it for three weeks. You don't know why.

[quick, factual] Here's what you don't know. A photo sent from a phone can carry the exact spot where it was taken. You check. Across the street.

[very quiet, dread building] You look through the blinds. A dark car. Engine running.

[sharp, a jolt] Then the headlights go off.

[hushed, frozen] You don't move. Neither does the car. For an hour, you stand in the dark, in his jacket, watching it watch you.

[tense, brisk] You don't sleep. At seven, the car is gone. There's an envelope in your mailbox. No stamp.

[reading, flat] A plane ticket. Cancún. Tomorrow. Your name. And a note.

[reading, cold, menacing] "Find your brother. Seven days. Or we collect from family."

[quiet, dawning horror] Marcus has no family left. Except you.

[cold, quiet] Day one starts now.

[steady, weighing it] You could call the FBI agent who took your statement. Agent Kerr. She's the only one who believed you. "Anytime," she said.

[low, uneasy] Or you could go. Nobody knows Marcus like you do.

[slow, building] You step outside for air. Across the street, the dark car is back.

[very quiet, chilling] And the window is rolling down.

[direct, tense] Call the FBI, or get on that plane?

[lighter, inviting] Comment CALL or GO. The majority writes Part 2.
