# Season 2, Part 1, "You Never Take It Off": image prompts

One still image per shot, in the 2D look of the reference reels (playbook §11.1). Shot numbers and times follow [script.md](script.md).

**How to use**
1. The reference images are in this part's [reference-images/](reference-images/) folder. Attach the ones listed under **Refs** to each prompt. **Always attach `you.png` whenever YOU is in the shot**, so he stays on-model.
2. Paste each prompt exactly as written. Every prompt is complete, with the style included.
3. Save each image in `part-01/images/` under the name shown in **Save as**. Type just the name (e.g. `04`), without `.png`.
4. **Text cards** need no image. I build them in the edit, as are all on-screen text, phone messages and the vote buttons.
5. Movement is added in the edit.

**This part's look:** night at home in blue-black, lit by one streetlight, the porch light and phone glow; then a gray, quiet morning. The dark car is always across the street. YOU wears the yellow jacket even in bed.

**To make:** 13 images, 2 text cards and 6 reused images (`01`, `02`, `03a`, `03b`, `03c` and `14` are already in `images/`, copied from Season 1).
