[low, hurt, steady] Your brother left you to drown. His first question was about the money.

[firm, steady] Most of you said test him.

[flat, quiet, lying] "I lost it," you say. "In the sea."

[cold, measured] Then he smiles. The same smile from that dinner on the ship.

[quiet, bitter] And nobody searches a man overboard.

[slow, hurt] You remember the foil blanket. The nurse. Nobody even touched your collar.

[urgent, scared, Marcus] "If it's gone, we're both dead."

[quiet, giving in] You look at your brother. Then you tap your head. "I remember them. All twelve."

[releasing, warm] He laughs. He hugs you.

[soft] For one second, he's just your brother again.

[cold, noticing] Then his hand goes straight to your collar. Feeling for the cut.

[urgent] Lucía runs out of the shop. "Someone's on the road."

[sharp, a jolt] Headlights. A car stops outside the dive shop.

[calm, official, reassuring] Agent Kerr gets out. "FBI. You're safe now. Come with me."

[low, urgent, Marcus] "Don't," Marcus whispers. "She's not here for you."

[quiet, torn] She's right. He did. And she's the only one who believed you.

[cold narrator, very quiet] What you can't see: her back seat. A man in a gray polo. He checks his steel watch.
