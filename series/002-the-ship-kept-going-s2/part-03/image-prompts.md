# Season 2, Part 3, "Rule Three": image prompts

One still image per shot, in the 2D look of the reference reels (playbook §11.1). Shot numbers and times follow [script.md](script.md). Written for **BURN winning**; for KEEP, shot 07 becomes the strip folded into a flip-flop (prompt at the bottom).

**How to use**
1. The reference images are in this part's [reference-images/](reference-images/) folder. Attach the ones listed under **Refs** to each prompt. **Always attach `you.png` whenever YOU is in the shot**, so he stays on-model.
2. Paste each prompt exactly as written. Every prompt is complete, with the style included.
3. Save each image in `part-03/images/` under the name shown in **Save as**. Type just the name, without `.png`.
4. **Text cards** need no image. I build them in the edit, as are all on-screen text and the vote buttons.
5. Movement is added in the edit.

**This part's look:** a cheap Cancún hotel room at night (one lamp, a ceiling fan, a sink); then Isla Mujeres in turquoise water and golden-hour sun; the pier at sunset. Lucía's red bandana is the only other strong color; nobody but YOU wears yellow.

**To make:** 16 images, 2 text cards and 2 reused images (`01a` and `01b` are Part 2's `08` and `06`, which I copy in once Part 2's images exist).
