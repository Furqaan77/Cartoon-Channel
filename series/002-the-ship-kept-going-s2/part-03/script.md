## Voiceover (paste into TTS)
<!-- VO:START -->
[low, intimate, unsettling] Your brother stitched two million dollars into your collar. And everyone knows you're wearing it.

[firm, steady] Most of you said burn it.

[quick, factual] Here's the problem. Short-term memory holds about seven things. You need twelve.

[slow, focused] So you sit up all night in a Cancún hotel, reading them again and again.

[tense, guilty] Rule three: never say the words out loud. You break it. You whisper them in the dark until they stick.

[very quiet, ominous] Kerr's phone lies on the nightstand. Screen down.

[brisk] 2 a.m. A match. The strip curls into the sink. The only copy is in your head now.

[knowing] Keep voters, paper can be stolen. A head can't. At least, that's what you think.

[brighter, moving] Morning. The ferry to Isla Mujeres. Four miles long. One road around it. Twenty minutes from Cancún. Nowhere to hide a stranger in a yellow jacket.

[curious, uneasy] At the dock, a woman with a red bandana is waiting. "You're the brother," she says. "He said you'd come in yellow."

[warm, quick] Her name is Lucía. She runs the dive shop. Marcus has been paying her to hide him.

[light, then pointed] "Leave the jacket here," she says. "He'll want it." You keep it on.

[cold narrator, crisp] Meanwhile, in Cancún, Agent Kerr steps off a plane. She didn't tell you she was coming.

[slowing, hopeful] Sunset. Lucía points to the end of her pier. A man in a navy shirt. His back to you.

[soft, emotional] From behind, he looks just like you.

[holding breath] He turns around. Marcus.

[quiet, hurt] Not "I'm sorry." Not "Are you okay?"

[cold, flat, Marcus] "Do you still have the collar?"

[direct, tense] Tell him the words, or test him first?

[lighter, inviting] Comment TRUST or TEST. The majority writes Part 4.
