# Season 2, Part 5, "Twelve Words" (finale): image prompts

One still image per shot, in the 2D look of the reference reels (playbook §11.1). Shot numbers and times follow [script.md](script.md). Written for **BROTHER winning**; for FBI, shot 03 is replaced (prompt at the bottom).

**How to use**
1. The reference images are in this part's [reference-images/](reference-images/) folder. Attach the ones listed under **Refs** to each prompt. **Always attach `you.png` whenever YOU is in the shot**, so he stays on-model.
2. Paste each prompt exactly as written. Every prompt is complete, with the style included.
3. Save each image in `part-05/images/` under the name shown in **Save as**. Type just the name, without `.png`.
4. **Text cards** need no image. I build them in the edit, as are all on-screen text, phone messages and the laptop screen.
5. Movement is added in the edit.

**This part's look:** black sea and starlight, like Season 1's first night; a small open dive boat; the laptop glow on two faces. The yellow jacket is the only color out there. Agent Kerr on the shore is lit only by her phone.

**To make:** 13 images, 4 text cards and 3 reused images (`04` is Part 4's `19`; `12` and `13` are Part 3's `07` and `06`, which I copy in once those exist).
