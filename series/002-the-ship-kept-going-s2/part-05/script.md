## Voiceover (paste into TTS)
<!-- VO:START -->
[low, tense, deliberate] The only copy of two million dollars is in your head. And everyone wants it.

[firm, urgent] Most of you said brother.

[fast, tense] You run. Lucía's boat. Marcus at the wheel. Kerr's headlights shrinking behind you.

[knowing, cold] FBI voters, check her back seat.

[quiet, uneasy] Black water again. Three weeks ago, it almost killed you. This time, your brother's in the boat.

[urgent, Marcus] Marcus opens a laptop. "Type them," he says. "Don't say them. Rule three."

[slow, careful] Twelve words. One by one. Your fingers are shaking. Marcus watches every key. He doesn't blink.

[holding breath] The screen loads.

[flat, stunned] Balance: zero. He refreshes. Zero.

[slow, cold] Moved this morning. At 2:14 a.m.

[sharp, Marcus] "Who else knew?" he says. "Who did you tell?"

[quiet, thinking] No one. You burned the strip. You never wrote them down.

[slowing, horror dawning] You only whispered them. In the dark. In a hotel room. Next to the phone Agent Kerr gave you.

[quick, factual] Here's what you don't know. A crypto transfer can't be reversed. Anyone can watch the money move. Nobody can see who moved it.

[cold, flat] Two million dollars. Gone in a second. And there's no one to call.

[quiet, heavy, Marcus] Marcus stops the engine. "Then they don't need me anymore," he says.

[slow, realizing] The people he robbed have their money back. Marcus was only alive because he had it.

[very quiet] He looks at you. "And they don't need you."

[low, building dread] Behind you, an engine. A boat. No lights. Coming fast.

[tense, quiet] Three weeks ago, a ship left you behind. This one is coming for you.

[cold narrator, slow] On the shore, Agent Kerr picks up her phone and types one word.

[whispered, final] "Done."

[lighter, inviting, a hint of a grin] Comment SEASON 3 if you want to know who's on that boat.
<!-- VO:END -->
