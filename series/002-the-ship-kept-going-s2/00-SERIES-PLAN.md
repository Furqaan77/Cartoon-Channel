## Logline
Three weeks after surviving the cruise ship, the killer is at your door. He thinks you're carrying your brother's $2 million. He's right. It's stitched into your collar.

## Series question
Can you find Marcus before the man from table nine finds the money, and who can you actually trust?

## Hidden truth (writer's eyes only)
Marcus turned the stolen $2 million into crypto. The only copy of the 12-word recovery phrase is handwritten on a strip of fabric stitched inside the collar of the yellow jacket. On the ship he couldn't carry it (the hitman would search his cabin and his body), so he made you carry it: a man overboard in bright yellow gets spotted, rescued, wrapped in a blanket and never searched. The 3:38 note was never mercy; it was a pickup. Marcus is hiding on Isla Mujeres, Mexico, waiting for the jacket to come to him. **FBI Agent Kerr, who took your statement in Season 1, is on the payroll of the people Marcus robbed.** She gave the man from table nine your address, she "advises" you to go to Mexico so you lead them to Marcus and the money, and the "secure" phone she gives you is listening. When you whisper the twelve words in the dark to memorize them, they hear every word, and at 2:14 a.m. the money is gone.

## Why you can't just leave or call the police
You do call the police: the FBI agent on your case. She *is* the trap. Doing nothing isn't an option either ("seven days, or we collect from family", and you're Marcus's only family). And the jacket that marks you is the thing everyone wants.

## Part-by-part breakdown

### Part 1: "You Never Take It Off"
- **Hook line:** "2:14 a.m. A photo of your front door."
- **Episode goal:** survive the night, and decide what to do.
- **Beats:** 1) the photo and "From behind…" → the S1 recap in three lines → 2) "You never take that jacket off." → the photo was taken from across the street; a car, engine running → 3) false relief: morning, the car's gone → an envelope: a ticket to Cancún and "Find your brother. Seven days. Or we collect from family." → 4) the car is back, and the window is rolling down.
- **Cliffhanger:** arrival.
- **CTA:** CALL (the FBI) or GO (to Mexico).
- **Loops:** opens L1, L2, L3.

### Part 2: "The Collar"
- **Hook line:** "A killer gave you seven days to find your brother."
- **Episode goal:** get to Mexico and find Marcus's trail.
- **Beats:** 1) the vote: Kerr says go, and sends a "secure" phone → 2) Cancún, 86 degrees, the only person in a jacket → 3) the collar's stitching doesn't match; twelve words in Marcus's handwriting → the fact: a recovery phrase moves money → "You weren't his decoy. You were his suitcase." → 4) a text: "Isla Mujeres. Come alone. Never say the words out loud. —M"
- **Cliffhanger:** reveal, then a message from nowhere.
- **CTA:** KEEP the strip, or BURN it (after memorizing).
- **Loops:** closes L1 · opens L4, L5.

### Part 3: "Rule Three"
- **Hook line:** "Your brother stitched two million dollars into your collar."
- **Episode goal:** reach Marcus without losing the words.
- **Beats:** 1) the vote: you memorize and burn it → the memory fact → you whisper the words all night, breaking rule three; the phone on the nightstand → 2) the ferry; the island fact → Lucía: "He said you'd come in yellow." "Leave the jacket here." → 3) meanwhile, Agent Kerr lands in Cancún without telling you → 4) false relief: the pier at sunset; Marcus turns around → his first words: "Do you still have the collar?"
- **Cliffhanger:** a reveal in one line (what he cares about).
- **CTA:** TRUST him (tell him the words) or TEST him (say you lost them).
- **Loops:** closes L2 · opens L3 more sharply.

### Part 4: "The Pier"
- **Hook line:** "Your brother left you to drown. His first question: the money."
- **Episode goal:** find out what Marcus really wants.
- **Beats:** 1) the vote: "I lost it." His face, for one second → 2) he confesses: the suitcase plan, the note was a pickup, the yellow was so they'd see you → 3) false relief: "I remember them." He hugs you, and his hand goes straight to the collar → 4) headlights: Agent Kerr. "You're safe now." Marcus: "Don't. She's not here for you." → what you can't see: the man from table nine in her back seat.
- **Cliffhanger:** dramatic irony.
- **CTA:** go with your BROTHER, or with the FBI.
- **Loops:** half-closes L3 and L4 · L5 still ticking.

### Part 5: "Twelve Words" (finale)
- **Hook line:** "The only copy of two million dollars is in your head."
- **Episode goal:** get the money somewhere safe, and stay alive.
- **Beats:** 1) the vote: Lucía's boat, out on black water again → 2) Marcus: "Type them. Don't say them." You type twelve words → balance: zero. Moved at 2:14 a.m. → 3) who knew? You never wrote them down. You only whispered them, next to Kerr's phone → the irreversible-transfer fact → 4) Marcus: "Then they don't need me anymore. And they don't need you." A boat with no lights → on the shore, Kerr types one word: "Done."
- **Cliffhanger:** final sting and callback.
- **CTA:** comment SEASON 3.
- **Loops:** closes L3, L4, L5 · leaves L6 (the boat) as the Season 3 door.

## Season 3 door
A boat with no lights is coming, the money is gone, and the only people who know what happened are the two brothers nobody needs anymore.

## Other premises considered
- **"The Other Passenger"** (A + C): the ship's manifest shows a passenger with your name who boarded a week before you. *Lost because:* it drops Marcus and the money, which Season 1 viewers came back for.
- **"The Trial"** (B + E): you testify against the man from table nine; every part is a day in court. *Lost because:* courtrooms are static on screen, and it can't use the outfit or the sea.
- **"The Ferry"** (A + B): you take the 11 a.m. ferry yourself and the killer is on it. *Lost because:* it repeats Season 1's trapped-on-a-boat shape too closely.
