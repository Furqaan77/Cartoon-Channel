## Character phrases (use these exact words in every prompt)

- **MARCUS:** `a stick-figure man with a white egg-shaped head, short neat black hair, a short black beard, a navy button-up shirt with rolled sleeves and a gold watch`
- **DOYLE (TABLE 9 MAN):** `a broad-shouldered stick-figure man with a bald white egg-shaped head, heavy black brows, a flat expressionless mouth, a gray polo shirt and a chunky steel dive watch on his left wrist`
- **AGENT KERR:** `a stick-figure woman with a white egg-shaped head, short gray-streaked hair, round reading glasses, tired eyes, a dark blazer over a white shirt and a blue ID lanyard`
- **LUCÍA:** `a stick-figure woman with a white egg-shaped head, long curly dark-brown hair tied back with a red bandana, a teal rash-guard top, khaki cargo shorts and a shark-tooth necklace`
- **THE BOAT WITH NO LIGHTS:** `a low matte-black speedboat with no lights at all and two big outboard motors`
- **THE CREW:** `two stick-figure men with white egg-shaped heads in black hooded rain jackets and black caps, their faces in shadow`
- **DOYLE'S BOAT:** `a battered white sport-fishing boat with a faded blue stripe, tall outriggers and a small cabin`
- **THE CAR:** `a dark gray sedan with tinted windows`
- **THE SECOND JACKET:** `a second bright yellow zip-up windbreaker with a high collar, soaked and dripping, its collar whole and uncut`. It's the one exception to "only YOU wears yellow", and it's never worn on screen.

Nobody but YOU wears yellow.

## Colour and light
- **At sea (Part 1):** black water, starlight, one flashlight beam; then a pink-gold dawn.
- **Isla Mujeres (Parts 2–4):** hard turquoise daylight, short black shadows; a stuffy police office with a ceiling fan; sunset orange and long blue shade where Doyle waits.
- **Puerto Morelos (Part 5):** sodium-orange dock lamps, black water, the leaning lighthouse against the stars.
- **Home (Part 5 sting):** blue-black, one streetlight, phone glow.
