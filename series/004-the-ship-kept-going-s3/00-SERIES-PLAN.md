## Logline
A boat with no lights takes your brother. By morning he's "drowned", his money is moving, and the man who pushed you off the ship needs you alive until Sunday.

## Series question
Is Marcus really dead, and who has his two million dollars?

## Hidden truth (writer's eyes only)
**Marcus moved the money himself.** He wrote the twelve words; he never needed you to remember them. At 2:14 a.m. on Wednesday he moved the $2M to a new wallet while you slept, and the "balance: zero" on Lucía's boat was a performance. Making you say the words out loud next to Kerr's phone was the cover: everyone, the bosses included, would blame the phone and the brother who whispered. **Agent Kerr is his partner.** She stayed on the bosses' payroll to steer them, but she sold out to Marcus for half. Her text "Done." called in the boat with no lights (her hired crew), Marcus stopped the engine so it could reach him, and he left you drifting with the key in his pocket. Then Kerr planted Marcus's **second yellow jacket** (he bought two before the cruise) in the water off Isla Mujeres so he'd be "presumed drowned". Her mistake: she dropped it south of the island, and the current runs north. Kerr's official report blames the "secure" phone in your hotel room, so the bosses now think **you** have the money. The man from table nine (Doyle) has failed them twice; they've given him until Sunday to bring back the money or "the man who lost it". The boat with no lights leaves Puerto Morelos at 2:14 a.m. Sunday, taking Marcus and Kerr away for good.

## Why you can't just go to the police
The FBI agent on the case *is* the cover-up. Her report already says Marcus drowned and the money left your hotel phone. The island police show you her signature (POLICE branch). Going home doesn't work either: the bosses think you have their $2M, and their man is standing next to you.

## World & recurring devices
- **Places:** the black sea at night; Doyle's rented sport-fishing boat at dawn; Lucía's pier and dive shop on Isla Mujeres (from Season 2); the island police office; the marina at **Puerto Morelos**, with its old leaning lighthouse (finale).
- **Clock:** **2:14 a.m.** stays the series' signature time. The money moved at 2:14 (S2 finale), Kerr's report says 2:14, and the boat leaves at 2:14 on Sunday.
- **Meter:** HUD **DAYS LEFT** to Doyle's Sunday deadline: Part 2 shows 3 (Thursday) → Part 3 2 (Friday) → Part 4 2 → 1 (Saturday) → Part 5 0 (Sunday). Part 1 has no meter; the deadline doesn't exist yet.
- **Rules:** continuing the numbering. Rule one: float (S1). Rule two: never chase what you can't catch (S1). Rule three: never say the words out loud (S2). **Rule four: the dead don't spend money** (Doyle, Part 2; it pays off in Parts 3 and 5).
- **Signature object:** the yellow jacket, and now there are **two**.
- **Repeated phrases:** "Rule four." (Doyle, Parts 2, 3, 5) · "The money, or the man who lost it." (Parts 2, 5) · "Three weeks ago, a ship left you behind." (S2 finale, now "Tonight, you leave him.") · **"Whatever happens, I love you, okay?"** (S1 Marcus; the season's last line).

## Part-by-part breakdown

### Part 1: "No Lights"
- **Hook line:** "Midnight. Black water. A boat with no lights, coming straight at you."
- **Episode goal:** get away from the boat.
- **Beats:** 1) the boat; recap in two lines; the engine is off and Marcus won't start it; the navigation-lights fact → 2) the boat drifts alongside; "Marcus. Come on."; he goes without a fight; the light goes off; the key is gone → 3) false relief: hours adrift, then dawn, a fishing boat, a towel, water → 4) a steel dive watch: the man from table nine. "Morning. Where's your brother?"
- **Cliffhanger:** reveal (the man from table nine).
- **CTA:** TELL him the truth, or LIE.
- **Loops:** half-closes L1 · opens L4.

### Part 2: "Rule Four"
- **Hook line:** "The man who pushed you off a ship just pulled you out."
- **Episode goal:** survive Doyle, and find out who took Marcus.
- **Beats:** 1) the vote; "Nobody took your brother. Not my people." → 2) his name is Doyle; he failed twice; "Until Sunday. The money, or the man who lost it."; it's Thursday (DAYS LEFT 3); false relief: until Sunday he needs you alive → 3) the public-wallet fact; the money moved again at 4:02 a.m.; "Rule four. The dead don't spend money." → 4) the radio: an American presumed drowned; divers found a yellow windbreaker. You're wearing it.
- **Cliffhanger:** impossible fact.
- **CTA:** go to the POLICE (he's alive), or stay QUIET and hunt him with Doyle.
- **Loops:** opens L2, L3.

### Part 3: "Against the Current"
- **Hook line:** "Your brother 'drowned.' His jacket washed up. You're wearing his jacket."
- **Episode goal:** find out whose jacket it is.
- **Beats:** 1) the vote; Lucía's pier; the jacket under her dock, collar uncut: his, not yours → 2) false relief: maybe he's alive; "He bought two." → 3) the current fact: the jacket came south, so somebody put it there; "Rule four."; Marcus wrote the words himself, so why make you say them? (DAYS LEFT 2) → 4) Lucía's photo: Marcus laughing with Agent Kerr at Puerto Morelos; then tires on gravel: Kerr's car.
- **Cliffhanger:** arrival (after a photo reveal).
- **CTA:** RUN, or STAY and face her.
- **Loops:** half-closes L2 · moves L3 · opens L5.

### Part 4: "Him or Me"
- **Hook line:** "Your brother faked his death. The FBI agent who helped him just pulled up."
- **Episode goal:** get away from Kerr and find Marcus before Sunday.
- **Beats:** 1) the vote; "I'm sorry for your loss."; Doyle's gone → 2) the police office: Kerr's report; the money left the phone in your hotel room at 2:14; the phone she gave you; "Who do you think they'll believe?" She lets you go, because she wants them to find you → 3) false relief: Lucía heard the harbor radio: the boat with no lights is at Puerto Morelos and leaves Sunday at 2:14 a.m. You're ahead for once → 4) Doyle steps out of the shade: "Saturday. One day left." (DAYS LEFT 1) "Take me to your brother. Or I take you."
- **Cliffhanger:** threat, then a decision.
- **CTA:** HIM or ME.
- **Loops:** moves L4 and L5 · L2 and L3 ready to close.

### Part 5: "Man Overboard" (finale)
- **Hook line:** "Sunday, 2:14 a.m. You just brought a killer to your brother."
- **Episode goal:** face Marcus.
- **Beats:** 1) the vote; the marina, the leaning lighthouse, the black boat; Marcus on deck, dry, not surprised (DAYS LEFT 0) → 2) "You wrote the words." "I moved it myself. At 2:14. While you slept." "So everyone would blame the phone. And you." → 3) Kerr with the laptop: "There's your man, Doyle. Yellow jacket."; the missing-for-years fact → 4) Doyle takes the laptop and Kerr: "The money, and the one who took it." To Marcus: "You drowned on Wednesday. Stay drowned." You walk away: "Three weeks ago, a ship left you behind. Tonight, you leave him." → sting: home, 2:14 a.m., a photo of your front door from across the street: "Whatever happens, I love you, okay?"
- **Cliffhanger:** final sting and callback.
- **CTA:** comment SEASON 4.
- **Loops:** closes L1–L5 · leaves L6 as the Season 4 door.

## Other premises considered
- **"The Bosses"** (A + B): the boat is the people Marcus robbed, and Season 3 is a hostage negotiation. *Lost because:* it closes the season door with the obvious answer, and it doesn't explain the hole viewers will spot (Marcus wrote the words, so why did he need you?).
- **"Kerr's Season"** (C + E): you go after Kerr alone and expose her. *Lost because:* it drops Marcus, the heart of the series, to the side.
- **"Two Jackets, Two Brothers"** (A + C): the bosses' men can't tell you apart and hunt you as Marcus. *Lost because:* it repeats Season 1's mistaken-identity shape. It survives here as the planted second jacket.
