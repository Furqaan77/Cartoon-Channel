## Logline
You're pushed off a cruise ship at 2:14 a.m. by a man who calls you by your brother's name. To survive, you have to outlast the ocean and then the truth.

## Series question
Will you survive, and why did your brother send you to that rail?

## Hidden truth (writer's eyes only)
Marcus, your older brother, stole $2 million from people who don't forget. They put a hitman on the cruise (the man from table 9) to make Marcus "fall overboard", because at sea a fall looks like an accident. Marcus planned this from the start. He invited you because you're his height. He told them he'd be at the deck 5 rail at 2 a.m. Then he put you in his signature yellow jacket, zipped his own wallet into the pocket ("if you washed up, you'd wash up as me"), and texted you to meet him there. He watched from deck 6. Then he couldn't let you drown, so at 3:38 he slid the anonymous "Man overboard. Deck five. 2:14." note under the security office door. The ferry ticket in the wallet is bait for the hitman. Marcus is already gone.

## Why you can't just get help
You're in the open sea at night. Nobody saw you go over, the ship is moving at 23 mph, and no one will miss you until breakfast. Later, once you're rescued, there's no camera footage and no other witness, and the man who pushed you claims he saw you slip.

## World & recurring devices
- **Ship:** the *Halcyon Bay* (fictional), 7-night Western Caribbean cruise out of Galveston. Night 3, sailing toward Cozumel (due 7 a.m.).
- **Place:** deck 5, at the back of the ship (the aft rail). Deck 6 is directly above.
- **Clock:** **2:14 a.m.** is the series' signature time. It's on screen in every part, and the finale's last scene happens at 2:14.
- **Meter:** **STRENGTH %** while you're in the water (Parts 1–4). FLOAT won Part 1, so: 100% → 94 → 88 → 85 at the end of Part 2. KEEP won Part 2, so Part 3 runs 85 → 84 → 79 → 70% (clock 3:08 → 4:31 a.m.). Part 4: 62% when the searchlight finds you, rescued at 5:20 a.m.; no meter after the rescue. The on-screen clock runs through every part.
- **Rules:** Rule one: float (Part 1). Rule two: never chase what you can't catch (Part 2).
- **Signature object:** Marcus's **bright yellow jacket**. It marks you for death, then saves you (rescuers spot the yellow).
- **Repeated phrases:** "Done." (the hitman's text) · "From behind, you really do look like him." (Part 4, then the finale's last line)

## Real facts used (checked; keep the wording hedged as written)
| Fact | Part |
|---|---|
| Deck 5 ≈ 50 ft up; you hit the water at almost 40 mph | 1 |
| Panic swimming exhausts you fast; "float first" is standard sea-survival advice | 1 |
| A cruise ship at ~20 knots ≈ 23 mph ≈ a third of a mile a minute | 1 |
| About 20 people a year go overboard from cruise ships; many aren't missed for hours | 1 |
| The fastest swimmers top out around 5 mph | 2 |
| From water level your horizon is ~1 mile away; a ship's high lights stay visible for ~45 min | 2 |
| Bright yellow and orange are among the easiest colours to spot from the air | 2 |
| In ~82°F water the cold won't kill you; exhaustion is the danger | 3 |
| You can trap air in clothing to help you float | 3 |
| Sargassum seaweed floats in mats in the Gulf and Caribbean | 3 |
| "Oscar" is the cruise-ship code for man overboard | 3 |
| Rescue teams search where the current would carry you, not where you fell | 4 |
| The 2010 US Cruise Vessel Security and Safety Act says ships should have overboard-detection tech "to the extent available"; many don't | 4 |
| The FBI takes statements from US citizens about serious crimes on cruise ships | 5 |

## Part-by-part breakdown

### Part 1: "Nobody Saw"
- **Hook line:** "You just fell off a cruise ship."
- **Episode goal:** survive the first minutes.
- **Beats:** 1) the fall and its facts → 2) instinct vs Rule one (float) → 3) the ship keeps going, and nobody will miss you until breakfast → 4) flashback: Marcus's text, he never showed, "You didn't slip." → "Bye, Marcus."
- **Cliffhanger:** impossible fact: "You're not Marcus."
- **CTA:** SWIM or FLOAT. Post-CTA tease: something is zipped inside the jacket.
- **Loops:** opens L1, L2, L5.

### Part 2: "Done"
- **Hook line:** "You were pushed off a cruise ship at 2:14 a.m."
- **Episode goal:** reach the ship, or stay alive without it.
- **Beats:** 1) the vote result plus the swim-speed fact → 2) Rule two, and the lights drop below the horizon → 3) the jacket: "freezing" at 82 degrees → 4) Marcus's wallet → 5) meanwhile, table 9 drops your flip-flop over the rail and texts "Done."
- **Cliffhanger:** dramatic irony: "Done."
- **CTA:** KEEP or DITCH the jacket.
- **Loops:** closes L2 · opens L3, L4.

### Part 3: "Oscar"
- **Hook line:** "A man pushed you off a cruise ship. Then he texted one word: 'Done.'"
- **Episode goal:** last the night.
- **Beats:** 1) the jacket-air trick, or ditching it → 2) the seaweed scare and a human moment ("Whatever happens, I love you, okay?") → 3) the ferry ticket in the wallet → 4) meanwhile, the note at 3:38 and "Oscar, Oscar, Oscar" → 5) the ship's lights come back.
- **Cliffhanger:** arrival plus dramatic irony: the killer is on the rescue ship.
- **CTA:** SIGNAL or HIDE.
- **Loops:** closes L5 (partly) · opens L6, L8.

### Part 4: "From Behind"
- **Hook line:** "At 2:14 a.m., a man pushed you off a cruise ship."
- **Episode goal:** be rescued, then find out what happened.
- **Beats:** 1) the rescue (the yellow sleeve) → 2) the 2010 law, and the camera "broken since Tuesday" → 3) Marcus is missing → 4) deck 6 footage: Marcus watched → 5) table 9 shows up as a "witness".
- **Cliffhanger:** threat: "Tell me, and you slipped. Lie, and you slip again."
- **CTA:** TELL or LIE.
- **Loops:** closes L5 · opens L7, L9.

### Part 5: "The Note" (finale)
- **Hook line:** "The man who pushed you off a cruise ship asked one question."
- **Episode goal:** survive the choice, then learn the truth.
- **Beats:** 1) the vote is played out, and table 9 leaves the ship → 2) Marcus's letter (the hidden truth) → 3) the gut punch: "He brought you because you're his height" → 4) the FBI: your word against his → 5) three weeks later, 2:14 a.m., a photo of your front door.
- **Cliffhanger:** final sting: "From behind, you really do look like him."
- **CTA:** comment SEASON 2.
- **Loops:** closes L1, L3, L4, L6, L8 · leaves L7 and L9 open as the Season 2 door.
