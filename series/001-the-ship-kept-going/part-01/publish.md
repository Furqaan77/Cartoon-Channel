Never post the twist ("You're not Marcus") in a title, caption or comment. It's the reason people watch to the end.

## Instagram Reels

**Caption** (paste as is; the first line is all most people see before "more")
```
Part 1/5 · You just fell off a cruise ship at 2:14 a.m. Nobody saw. 🌊

The ship didn't stop. SWIM after it, or FLOAT and save your strength?

👇 Comment SWIM or FLOAT. The majority writes Part 2.
Follow so you don't miss Part 2 tomorrow.

#MidnightParts #TheShipKeptGoing #survivalstory #cruiseship #mysterystory
```

**Pinned comment** (post it yourself right after publishing, then pin it)
```
🗳️ Votes close in 24 hours: SWIM or FLOAT?
And who do you think "Marcus" really is? 👀
```

**Before you post**
- Instagram → Settings → **Media quality** → turn on **Upload at highest quality**. Without it, Instagram compresses the reel more.
- Cover: **Edit cover → Add from camera roll →** `cover.jpg`.
- After posting: **Pin to profile grid** (Part 1 stays pinned for the whole series).

## YouTube Shorts

**Title** (≤100 characters)
```
You Fell Off a Cruise Ship… and Nobody Saw 🌊 The Ship Kept Going, Part 1
```
Other options to test:
- `Nobody Saw You Fall Off the Cruise Ship. Swim or Float? (Part 1)`
- `2:14 AM. You Fell Overboard. The Ship Didn't Stop. | Part 1`

**Description**
```
Part 1 of 5. You just fell off a cruise ship at 2:14 a.m. Nobody saw, and the ship didn't stop.

SWIM after it, or FLOAT and save your strength? Comment your vote. The majority decides what happens in Part 2.

The Ship Kept Going is an interactive survival mystery. A new part every day, and you choose what happens next.
🔔 Subscribe so you don't miss Part 2.

#TheShipKeptGoing #MidnightParts #survival #mystery #shorts
```
The first three hashtags show above the title, so the series tag goes first.

**Tags** (Studio → Show more → Tags)
```
midnight parts, the ship kept going, cruise ship, man overboard, fell off a cruise ship, survival story, survival tips, mystery story, interactive story, choose what happens, animated story, what would you do, swim or float
```

**Pinned comment**
```
🗳️ SWIM or FLOAT? Reply with your vote. Votes close in 24 hours.
And who do you think "Marcus" really is? 👀
```

**Settings**
- Audience: **No, it's not made for kids**.
- Category: **Film & Animation**.
- Altered or synthetic content: **No**. Animated, clearly unrealistic content doesn't need the label.
- Create a playlist, **The Ship Kept Going (Full Series)**, and add this Short.
- When Part 2 is live, edit Part 1 → **Related video** → Part 2. Viewers then get a tap-through link from Part 1 to Part 2.
