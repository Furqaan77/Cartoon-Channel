# The Ship Kept Going: reference images and look

The look copies the reference reels (@saveo.house, @beanie.escape):
- 2D hand-drawn cartoon with bold black ink outlines.
- Stick-figure characters with smooth white egg-shaped heads and thin black limbs, set against richly detailed, moody backgrounds.
- One still image per beat (a new image every 3–6 seconds), with plain text cards in between.
- Vertical 9:16, like saveo. Beanie's 16:9 frame wastes the screen.

Generate these images **once**, before any part. Save each one in this folder under the filename shown. Attach them as references whenever a part's prompt lists them under **Refs**, so faces, clothes and places stay identical across all 5 parts.

## Colour and light
- **Night sea:** deep navy and teal, with sodium-orange ship lights. The yellow jacket is the brightest, most saturated thing in every frame. It's the series' accent color, the way pink is saveo's.
- **Flashbacks:** warm amber lamp or candle light.
- **Ship offices and the medical bay:** cold fluorescent white-green.
- **Daytime (Cozumel):** bright sun, turquoise water, slightly washed out.

## On-screen text and text cards (built in the edit, never by the image model)
These follow the saveo reels.
- **Captions:** bold white sans-serif, 1–3 words at a time, at about 70% of the frame height. The current key word or number is yellow (`#FFC72C`).
- **HUD:** two dark rounded boxes at the top with tiny gray labels, **TIME** (e.g. 2:14 AM) and **STRENGTH** (e.g. 100% with a yellow bar). They appear once you're in the water, and the values update as the script says.
- **Cream card** (`#F2ECE0`): a small letter-spaced monospace label at the top, then a big bold black number or line, then one short line. The key word sits on a yellow highlighter swipe.
- **Dark card** (`#15120F`): a small yellow monospace label (e.g. RULE 1), a bold white line, and a tiny gray line under it.
- **Vote result card** (cream): "PART N · YOU VOTED", with two bars and percentages. The winner's bar is yellow, the loser's black.
- **Vote buttons:** the last image of each part gets two pill buttons (one white, one yellow), with "PART N+1 TOMORROW · THE MAJORITY DECIDES" in small monospace above them.
- **Label chips:** small dark pills with white text and one yellow word (e.g. MARCUS'S **WALLET**, TABLE **9**).
- **Phone screens:** generate them blank. The message bubble goes on in the edit.
