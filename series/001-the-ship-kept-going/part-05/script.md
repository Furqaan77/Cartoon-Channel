## Voiceover (paste into TTS)
<!-- VO:START -->
[low, tense, deliberate] The man who pushed you off a cruise ship asked one question. Where is your brother going?

[steady, serious] Most of you said tell him.

[quiet, resigned] "The eleven o'clock ferry," you say. "Playa del Carmen."

[smooth, satisfied, cold] He smiles. "Feel better."

[flat, watching him go] And he walks out.

[brisk, time-stamped] 10:02 a.m. The ship docks in Cozumel. He's first down the gangway.

[slowing, uneasy] Back in your cabin, there's an envelope under the door. Marcus's handwriting.

[reading a letter, tired, ashamed, low] "I stole two million dollars from people who don't forget. They put a man on this ship to make me fall. Out here, a fall looks like an accident."

[reading, quieter, guilty] "So I gave them one. I told them I'd be at the rail at two."

[reading, cold honesty] "You're my height. In my jacket, in the dark, from behind, you're me. I put my wallet in the pocket. If you washed up, you'd wash up as me."

[reading, voice cracking] "I watched. I counted to sixty. I couldn't let you drown. So I wrote the note."

[reading, final, distant] "The ferry ticket was for them. I'm already gone."

[slow, hurt, piecing it together] He didn't bring you on this trip to celebrate. He brought you because you're his height.

[cold realization, measured] And when you told that man about the ferry, you didn't betray your brother. You did exactly what he planned.

[direct, knowing] Lie voters, you'd have protected him. He'd already protected himself. With you.

[serious, sober] Back in Galveston, an FBI agent takes your statement. No camera. No other witness. And a man who swears you slipped.

[tired, honest] "It's your word against his," she says.

[quiet, time passing] Three weeks later.

[hushed, tense, slowing right down] 2:14 a.m. Your phone lights up. Unknown number. A photo of your front door. Then one line.

[whispering, cold, menacing] "From behind, you really do look like him."

[lighter, inviting, a hint of a grin] Comment SEASON 2 if you want to find Marcus first.
<!-- VO:END -->
