## Voiceover (paste into TTS)
<!-- VO:START -->
[low, tense] You were pushed off a cruise ship at 2:14 a.m. And the man who did it called you Marcus.

[calm, approving] Most of you said float. Smart.

[cool, factual] Swim voters, here's why. The fastest swimmer alive tops out around five miles an hour. That ship is doing twenty-three. Ten minutes of chasing it, and you'd have burned a third of your strength for nothing.

[slow, rueful] Rule two: never chase what you can't catch.

[quiet, steady] So you lie back. You slow your breathing. And you watch it go.

[quiet, explaining, awe creeping in] From the water, your horizon is barely a mile away. The ship's lights sit high, so you'll see them for about forty-five minutes.

[soft, heavy, isolated] At 2:59, they drop below the edge of the world. Now it's just you and the stars.

[uneasy, remembering] Floating gives you time to notice things. Like the jacket. It's Marcus's jacket. Bright yellow. He made you wear it.

[imitating a brother, casual, warm] "It's freezing up there," he said.

[flat, pointed] It was eighty-two degrees.

[tense, slowing, discovering] Something hard presses into your ribs. An inside pocket. Zipped. Marcus's wallet. His license. His ship card. Three hundred dollars in cash.

[confused, unsettled] Why would Marcus zip his own wallet into a jacket, then put it on you?

[shift to cold narrator, measured] Meanwhile, back on deck five, a man in a gray polo picks up your flip-flop.

[low, suspicious] You've seen him before. Table nine. Every night. Alone. Never ordered dessert.

[slow, deliberate, each action a beat] He drops your flip-flop over the rail. Checks his watch. Takes out his phone. And types one word.

[whispered, final] "Done."

[direct, urgent] That soaked jacket is heavy, and floating in it is getting harder. But from the air, bright yellow is one of the easiest things to spot.

[lighter, inviting] Keep it, or ditch it? Comment KEEP or DITCH. The majority writes Part 3.
<!-- VO:END -->

**Optional, if Part 1 gets a great real comment:** add it after "Most of you said float. Smart." as `[amused] One of you wrote: "..."`. Never invent a comment.
