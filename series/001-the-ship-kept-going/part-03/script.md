## Voiceover (paste into TTS)
<!-- VO:START -->
[low, intimate, unsettling] A man pushed you off a cruise ship. Then he texted one word: "Done." He thinks you're dead.

[steady, approving] Most of you said keep the jacket. Good.

[teasing, knowing] Ditch voters, that yellow is going to matter.

[quick, instructive, like a survival tip] Here's a trick most people never learn. Zip it to your chin. Blow air down the collar. The back puffs up like a pillow. Now it holds you up while you rest.

[calm, then darker] At eighty-two degrees, the cold won't kill you. Exhaustion will.

[hushed, tense, slowing] 3:20 a.m. Something brushes your leg. Then again. You kick, and it wraps around your ankle.

[releasing tension, almost a laugh] Seaweed. Floating mats of it, for miles.

[cracking, emotional] You laugh, and it comes out as a sob.

[soft, nostalgic, wistful] You think about dinner. Marcus raised his glass and said,

[warm, a little too sincere] "Whatever happens, I love you, okay?"

[quiet, uneasy] You thought he was drunk.

[curious, slowing down] You open his wallet again. Behind his license, there's a folded ticket. Ferry. Cozumel to Playa del Carmen. Today, eleven a.m.

[flat, ominous] One passenger.

[shift to cold narrator, crisp] Meanwhile, on the ship, it's 3:38. Someone slides a note under the security office door.

[reading, clipped] "Man overboard. Deck five. 2:14." No name.

[low, pointed] Whoever wrote it knew the exact minute you fell.

[building urgency] Six minutes later, every speaker on the ship crackles.

[announcer, calm but urgent] "Oscar. Oscar. Oscar."

[quick, factual] On cruise ships, Oscar is the code for man overboard.

[rising hope, breathless] 4:31 a.m. Lights on the horizon. Getting bigger. The ship is coming back for you. Searchlights sweep the water.

[hope draining to dread, slow] But the man from table nine is on that ship. Right now, he's probably at the rail, watching the search.

[very quiet, chilling] If he sees you alive, "Done" isn't done.

[direct, tense] Signal the ship, or hide in the dark and wait for daylight?

[lighter, inviting] Comment SIGNAL or HIDE. The majority writes Part 4.
<!-- VO:END -->

**Optional, if Part 2 gets a great real comment:** add it after "Most of you said keep the jacket. Good." as `[amused] One of you wrote: "..."`. Never invent a comment. A real percentage can replace "Most of you" the same way.
