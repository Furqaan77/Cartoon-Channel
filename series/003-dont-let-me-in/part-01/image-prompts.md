# Part 1, "Let Me In": image prompts

One still image per shot, in the house look made darker for horror (see [reference/character-prompts.md](../reference/character-prompts.md)). Shot numbers follow [script.md](script.md). This is Part 1, so there's no vote branch.

**How to use**
1. The reference images are in this part's [reference-images/](reference-images/) folder. Attach the ones listed under **Refs** to each prompt. **Always attach `you.png` whenever YOU is in the shot, and `you-wet.png` for the copy.**
2. Paste each prompt exactly as written. Every prompt is complete, with the style included.
3. Save each image in `part-01/images/` under the name shown in **Save as**.
4. All on-screen text, the doorbell-camera look, captions and vote buttons are added in the edit, never by the image model.

**This part's look:** blue-black night inside the cabin, one small lamp. The doorbell-camera shots are a high, slightly fish-eye view of the porch, made gray in the edit. The two sunset flashbacks (04, 05) are warm orange. The copy is YOU, soaked, and slightly wrong.

**To make:** 16 images and 2 reused images.
