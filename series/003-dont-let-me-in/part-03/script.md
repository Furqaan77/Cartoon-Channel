## Voiceover
<!-- VO:START -->
[low, tense] You chose pull.

[fast, sharp] You grab her arm and drag her back. Slam the window. Below it, your voice. Muffled.

{COPY} [gentle, coaxing, through glass] "Ask him to open the door, Lily."

[slow, cold] Not "let me in." Ask him. It needs you to do it.

{LILY} [turning, sweet] "He says he's cold. Can we let him in?"

[firm, quick] You lock the window. You call Pruitt back.

{PRUITT} [heavy, slow] "Summer of eighty-seven, my wife Ruth went swimming after dark. Came home at two a.m. I let her in."

{PRUITT} [quiet, haunted] "She never dried. Ten days later, the divers found the real Ruth. Under my boathouse."

(pause 0.6)

{PRUITT} [urgent] "Who touched the water tonight? After dark?"

[realizing, quiet] You did. Up to your knee. Carrying Lily.

{PRUITT} [grim] "Then that thing is you. And it wants your place."

{RUTH} [distant, sleepy, from another room] "Walt? Come back to bed."

{PRUITT} [hushed, quick] "Rule three. It never dries."

[tense, then relieved] You check your leg. Dry. You're you.

(pause 0.8)

[barely a whisper] Then you see the footprints. Small. Wet. From Lily's room to the front door.

[very slow] She's standing there. Hand on the bolt.

{LILY} [calm, sweet] "He says there's room for both of you."

[direct, tense] Grab her, or talk her away from the door?

[lighter, direct] Comment GRAB or TALK.
