## Logline
At 2:47 a.m., someone knocks on your family's lake cabin door, using your voice. He's soaked. And your little sister can't tell which of you is real.

## Series question
What came out of the lake, and what does it want inside the cabin?

## Hidden truth (writer's eyes only)
- **What it is:** something in Hollow Lake copies anyone who touches the water after dark. A copy is always wet and never dries, and it can only say words it heard near the water.
- **The rule it lives by:** a copy can't enter a home unless a real person who lives there lets it in. Once it's let in, it takes that person's place. The real one is kept under the boathouse until sunrise; after sunrise, the lake keeps them. If a copy hasn't been let in by sunrise, it falls back into lake water.
- **The truth about Lily:** at sunset, Lily fell asleep on the dock with her feet in the lake. **The Lily you carried inside was already the copy**, and carrying her in counted as letting her in. The real Lily is under the boathouse.
- **What the copy outside wants:** the wet "you" on the porch is a second copy, made when you stepped in to fetch her. Copy-Lily can't let it in, because her invitation doesn't count, so all night she works on getting *you* to open a door.

## Why they can't just leave or call the police
- Your parents took the only car to a wedding two hours away.
- The cabin is on a dirt road around the lake, with no cell signal past the porch.
- The landline reaches 911, but the deputy is 50 minutes out on the lake road. Sunrise (5:51) comes sooner, and Pruitt says it only matters whether you open a door.
- Leaving means walking past the water in the dark, with a child, while *you* are standing on the porch.

## World & recurring devices
- **Place:** the family cabin on Hollow Lake: porch, dock, boathouse.
- **Clock:** 2:47 a.m. → sunrise at **5:51 a.m.** A "SUNRISE IN" countdown chip appears from Part 2.
- **The doorbell camera:** a gray, grainy feed with a "FRONT DOOR · REC" chip. It's how we see the copy without opening the door.
- **Rules (Pruitt's):**
  1. It can't come in unless someone who lives there lets it in.
  2. It only says words it heard by the water.
  3. It never dries.
- **Repeated phrase:** "Let's get you inside." It's what you said to Lily at sunset (Part 1), the copy's line all night, and in Part 5 you say it to the real Lily under the boathouse.
- **Repeated question:** "Which one of you is real?" Lily asks it in Part 1, and it means something new in Part 4.

## Part-by-part breakdown

### Part 1: "Let Me In"
- **Hook line:** "2:47 a.m. Someone knocks. And they're using your voice."
- **Episode goal:** keep Lily away from the door until you know who it is.
- **Beats:**
  1. The knock and your own voice, while you're inside. The doorbell camera shows you, soaked.
  2. Setup in 3 lines: the lake cabin, your parents away, you're watching Lily. At sunset you carried her in from the dock.
  3. Lily on the stairs, her hair still wet. The copy calls her with your sunset words. Lily asks, "Which one of you is real?"
  4. 911: no signal. Then the knocking stops and the porch is empty (false relief).
  5. The back door handle starts to turn.
- **Cliffhanger (arrival → decision):** "Did you lock that one?"
- **Vote:** LOCK (run to the back door) or HIDE (take Lily upstairs).
- **Loops:** opens L1, L2 and L3.

### Part 2: "Rule One"
- **Hook line:** "You chose lock."
- **Episode goal:** hold the doors until help, or until morning.
- **Beats:**
  1. The vote's consequence: you reach the back door and see your own dripping face against the glass. It says "Let's get you inside." again, like a recording.
  2. The landline rings. It's Walt Pruitt across the lake: "Who's standing on your porch?" He gives rules 1 and 2 (closes L2).
  3. Sunrise is 5:51 and it's 3:20. Locked doors, two and a half hours: you can do that (false relief).
  4. Upstairs, Lily is talking to someone. "Okay. I'll ask him." Her window is open, and the copy is below it, looking up.
- **Cliffhanger (overheard → decision):** her open window.
- **Vote:** PULL (drag her away from the window) or LISTEN (hear what it's telling her).
- **Loops:** closes L2 · opens L4.

### Part 3: "It Never Dries"
- **Hook line:** "You chose listen." (PULL: "You chose pull.")
- **Episode goal:** find out what it wants, and keep Lily from helping it.
- **Beats:**
  1. Vote consequence. LISTEN: you hear the copy below say, in your voice, "Ask him to open the door." PULL: you slam the window, and Lily says it anyway. Either way, Lily: "He says he's cold. Can we let him in?" (closes L4).
  2. You call Pruitt back. He tells you about Ruth in '87: he let her in, she never dried, and they found the real Ruth under his boathouse, ten days too late.
  3. Pruitt: "Who touched the water after dark?" You did, when you stepped in to fetch Lily. The thing outside is your copy, and it wants your place (closes L1). Behind him, a woman's voice: "Walt, come back to bed." (plant, opens L6)
  4. Pruitt: "Rule three: it never dries." You check your leg: dry. Relief.
  5. Small wet footprints run from Lily's room to the front door. Lily is standing there with her hand on the bolt.
- **Cliffhanger (threat → decision):** "He says there's room for both of you."
- **Vote:** GRAB (pull her off the door) or TALK (calmly talk her away from it).
- **Loops:** closes L1 and L4 · opens L5 and L6.

### Part 4: "Which One Is Real"
- **Hook line:** "You chose grab." (TALK: "You chose talk.")
- **Episode goal:** keep the door shut and keep Lily safe.
- **Beats:**
  1. GRAB: her hand is ice-cold and wet. TALK: she turns and her hair drips on the floor. Either way, she slides the bolt open and **nothing happens**. The copy outside doesn't move.
  2. Lily: "It doesn't work when I do it. It has to be you." Then: "Which one of you is real? I was just checking." (closes L3)
  3. The reversal: you carried *her* in at sunset. The real Lily has been under the boathouse since then (reopens L5 at full strength).
  4. Pruitt: "Sunrise, and the lake keeps her." It's 5:21. You have to get past the copy on the porch, and opening a door is enough to let it in.
  5. The porch is empty. Wet footprints lead down to the boathouse.
- **Cliffhanger (dramatic irony → decision):** it's going to the boathouse too.
- **Vote:** WINDOW (climb out of the window and run for it) or BOAT (call Pruitt to row over; slower, but he knows the lake).
- **Loops:** closes L3 · L5 at its peak.

### Part 5: "Sunrise"
- **Hook line:** "You chose window." (BOAT: "You chose boat.")
- **Episode goal:** reach the real Lily before 5:51.
- **Beats:**
  1. Vote consequence, then the boathouse. Under the boards, in the black water, is a small hand. You pull the real Lily up, freezing and alive.
  2. The copy of you blocks the boathouse door: "Let's get you inside." You say it too, to Lily, and it means something different from you.
  3. 5:51. The sun hits the lake. The copy and the Lily in the cabin collapse into lake water: two puddles, and a strand of weed.
  4. Morning. Mom calls: "Honey? Everything okay?" Lily's hair is dry (false relief, then the sting).
- **Sting (reveal):** Walt Pruitt waves from across the lake. Next to him, his wife Ruth waves too, and her hair is dripping (closes L6 as the Season 2 door).
- **CTA:** "Comment what you'd have done at the door. Follow for the next story."

## Other premises considered
- **Night-shift motel with rules.** Strong rules, but a weak reason not to just leave, and YOU's outfit fits a lake cabin better.
- **A voicemail from tomorrow.** A great hook, but the hidden truth is hard to keep fair across 5 parts.
- **The Babysitter.** Too close to the reference's "the trusted adult is the monster".
