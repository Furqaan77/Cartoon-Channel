# Sound library

These sound effects and music are shared by every part of every series. Each one is generated **once** with ElevenLabs and then reused. Never regenerate a sound that's already here. Only generate a new one when a scene needs something the library doesn't have, then add it to the table.

- Sound effects: `library/sfx/<name>.mp3`
- Music: `library/music/<name>.mp3`
- Status: ⬜ to make · ✅ in the folder
- **Loop** means a background sound that the edit repeats under a whole scene. It's generated at 20s and cut so it loops seamlessly.

## Mix levels (the same in every part)
`tools/build_part.py` first matches every sound to the voiceover's loudness, so the `gain` values in a part's `edit.json` are relative to the voice:
- Voiceover: 0 dB, always on top
- Music: −9 dB, ducked a further 8 dB automatically while the voice speaks (≈ −17 dB under the voice)
- Ambience loops (ocean, wind, crowd, engine): −14 to −18 dB
- Hits (impact, stinger, whoosh, splash): −2 to −8 dB, placed on the word they punctuate
- UI sounds (pop, beep, thump, ding): −6 to −12 dB
- The final mix is normalised to −14 LUFS, −1.5 dBTP (Instagram / Shorts)
